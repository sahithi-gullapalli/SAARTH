# Acme Corp Employee Policy

Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave of up to 5 days can be carried over to the next year.

The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Remote work is allowed up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of purchase through the finance portal, with receipts attached. Claims above 500 USD require director approval.

The probation period for new employees is 6 months. Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December.
